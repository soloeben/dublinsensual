Tickets

How can I buy tickets?

Use the Tickets page. Eventbrite and Go&Dance are the two official purchase paths currently linked by DSF.

Which ticket platform should I use?

You can choose either official provider. Check the live listing for the current ticket type, price, inclusions, availability and provider terms before checkout.

Are discounts available for international guests?

International guests may email proof of travel to admin@dublinsensual.com to ask whether travel support is currently available. No discount is guaranteed until the team confirms it.

Dates + venues

What are the 2026 festival dates?

The prep party is Thursday 29 October 2026. The main festival runs Friday 30 October, Saturday 31 October and Sunday 1 November 2026.

Where does each day take place?

Thursday is at Wigwam, Friday and Saturday are at BPM Studio, and Sunday is at Lost Lane. See addresses and map links on the Venue page.

Can DSF help with accommodation?

The festival tries to connect guests with local hosts where possible. Email admin@dublinsensual.com for current guidance.

Programme

Which dance styles are included?

The current festival information lists Bachata and Salsa, with Kizomba Fusion also referenced in the weekend styles.

Is the 2026 schedule final?

No. The detailed 2026 timetable is still being finalised. The Schedule page clearly separates the 2026 status from the 2025 archive.

What is the Jack & Jill competition?

It is a social dance competition with partners drawn rather than pre-selected. The current site lists Novice and Advanced divisions; 2026 timings and final rules will be published with the schedule.

Partners

Can my company sponsor DSF?

Yes. See the Sponsorship page and email admin@dublinsensual.com to discuss a proposal.
